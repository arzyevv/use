Use

A minimal browser for macOS. The page gets the whole window; everything else stays out of the way.

Download for Mac

Apple Silicon · macOS 15.4 or later · Free

Open the disk image and drag Use to Applications. The first time, macOS asks you to confirm it under System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway. Updates are built in.
